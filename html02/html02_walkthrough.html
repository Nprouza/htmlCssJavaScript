Recipe: Best Brownies

Soft, chewy brownies with chocolate chips

Prep time 15 minutes
Bake time 25 minutes
Makes 8 Brownies

These brownies are Best Enjoyed warm.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cup flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 tsp coco powder
  • 1 cup chocp;ate chip
  • 1/2 oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350F°
  2. Mix all Ingredients
  3. Put in oven
