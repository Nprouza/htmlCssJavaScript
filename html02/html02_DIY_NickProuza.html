Brownie Recipe

Soft, filled with chocolate, Best when warm!

Prep time 20 minutes
Bake time 15-20 minutes (Depends how gooey you want the brownies) Makes Full 8x10 inch baking tray

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs + Water
  • Powderded sugar
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • oil
  • vanilla Extract
  • Chocolate chips
