Brownie Recipe

Soft, filled with chocolate, Best when warm!

Prep time 20 minutes
Bake time 15-20 minutes (Depends how gooey you want the brownies)
Makes Full 8x10 inch baking tray

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs + 3/4 cup Water
  • 1/4 cup Powderded sugar
  • 1 tbsp Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla Extract
  • 2 cup Chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. set oven to 375f°
  2. Mix all ingredients in a bowl
  3. Grab a 8x10 or 8x8 baking tray and spread butter on the tray
  4. Put mixed ingredients into a 8x10 baking sheet or 8x8
  5. Put in oven for 15-20 minutes
  6. Take out of oven and Enjoy!!

Nutrition facts (per 28 grams of brownie)
