What Is the Box Model?

Every element is a box with four layers: content, padding, border, and margin. Padding is space inside the border. Margin is space outside the border.

Box 3: Large

Why Spacing Matters

Good spacing makes a page easy to read. Padding keeps text away from the edge of its box. Margin keeps boxes away from each other.

When a page looks crowded, add padding and margin. When a page looks wrong and you can't tell why, turn on the X-ray trick and look at the red lines.
